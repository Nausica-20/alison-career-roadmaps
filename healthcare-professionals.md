# 🏥 Healthcare Professionals

[← Back to All Roadmaps](../README.md)

## 📋 Career Overview

Provide medical care, patient support, and health services across various specialties including nursing, patient care, and healthcare administration.

### Salary Expectations
- **Entry Level:** $35,000 - $55,000/year
- **Mid Level:** $55,000 - $85,000/year
- **Senior Level:** $85,000 - $120,000+/year

### Job Market Outlook
📈 **Excellent** - Healthcare roles continue to grow with aging populations.
