## 📋 Career Overview

Oversee projects across technology, construction, and business sectors to ensure timely and budget-conscious delivery.

### Salary Expectations
- **Entry Level:** $$60,000 - $85,000/year
- **Mid Level:** $$85,000 - $120,000/year
- **Senior Level:** $$120,000 - $160,000+/year

### Job Market Outlook
📈 **Very Good**
