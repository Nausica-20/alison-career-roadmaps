# 🤖 Robotics Engineer

[← Back to All Roadmaps](../README.md)

## 📋 Career Overview

Design and program robots for manufacturing, healthcare, and service industries.

### Salary Expectations
- **Entry Level:** $45,000 - $70,000/year
- **Mid Level:** $70,000 - $100,000/year
- **Senior Level:** $100,000 - $150,000+/year

### Job Market Outlook
📈 **Good to Excellent** - Growing demand in this field.
