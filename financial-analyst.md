# 💰 Financial Analyst

[← Back to All Roadmaps](../README.md)

## 📋 Career Overview

Provide investment guidance and financial planning services by analyzing financial data and market trends.

### Salary Expectations
- **Entry Level:** $$55,000 - $75,000/year
- **Mid Level:** $$75,000 - $105,000/year
- **Senior Level:** $$105,000 - $145,000+/year

### Job Market Outlook
📈 **Good**
