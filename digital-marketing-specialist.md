# 📱 Digital Marketing Specialist

[← Back to All Roadmaps](../README.md)

## 📋 Career Overview

**Digital Marketing Specialists** manage online campaigns, SEO, content marketing, and social media strategies to drive brand awareness and business growth.

### Salary Expectations
- **Entry Level:** $45,000 - $65,000/year
- **Mid Level:** $65,000 - $90,000/year
- **Senior Level:** $90,000 - $130,000+/year

### Job Market Outlook
📈 **Excellent** - Digital marketing continues to grow as businesses prioritize online presence.
